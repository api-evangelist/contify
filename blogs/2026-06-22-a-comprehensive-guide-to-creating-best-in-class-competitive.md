Summary A best-in-class CI report should answer ‘what does it mean for stakeholders’, ‘what to do next’, and ‘what is likely to happen’, instead of just focusing on ‘what has happened’. CI reports are evolving from static, reactive documents into dynamic, prescriptive, continuously updated, and source-verified intelligence dashboards. Purpose-built AI intelligence platforms are enabling this shift.
